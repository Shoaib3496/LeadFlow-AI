# 🚀 LeadFlow AI

> AI-Powered Lead Discovery, Qualification, Ranking & CRM Platform

LeadFlow AI is an AI-powered lead generation and CRM platform that automatically discovers potential business opportunities from multiple online sources, filters irrelevant opportunities, evaluates leads using AI, ranks them based on commercial value, and provides a centralized CRM dashboard for managing qualified leads.

---

## 📌 Overview

Finding high-quality business opportunities manually across multiple platforms is time-consuming.

LeadFlow AI automates this process through a complete lead intelligence pipeline:
